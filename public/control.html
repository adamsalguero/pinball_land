Kiosk control

Displays

Three screens share one rotation: logos, the 2-story arcade, Outdoor Oasis, Flexible Event Options, the pinboard, and videos. Auto puts every TV back on that rotation. A button on one screen holds that TV.

Theme

Ron turns a party theme on from this iPad. It does not follow the calendar. Halloween and Christmas also play family clips from those media folders.

Pinball collection

Leaderboards

Each real pinball machine has a board. Scores sort highest first. Archive keeps a board. Delete removes it.

Score submissions

Guests send a name and score. Approve it before it lands on the board.

Archived boards

Live pinboard

Guests post from the Pinball Land app with no PIN. Approve them here unless auto-approve is on.

Public access

Guests open a link from outside the venue WiFi. Turn this on after the one-time Tailscale setup. The wall QR then carries a daily code. Phones on the venue WiFi need no code.

Pinboard photos

Off hides the wall pinboard, the QR card, and guest photo upload.

Allow admin from internet

AirPlay

One tap picks the TV. When an iPhone connects, that screen leaves rotation and comes back when the mirror ends.

Clock

Shown under the time. Leave the default blank to show only the time.

Videos

Upload mp4, m4v, webm, mov, or mkv. Halloween and Christmas cartoons play from the local cache after the PC downloads them. The default theme has no video card.

YouTube

Only links you paste play. The wall uses the privacy embed and skips videos that cannot be embedded.

Smart plugs

Kasa plugs on the TV power cords only. Set each BenQ Switch on state to On. Never put the PC on a plug.

Transitions

A short crossfade between cards, or off. Screens change a few seconds apart.

Updates

At sign-in the PC checks GitHub main before the server starts, then again at this time. A bad update rolls back. Scores, photos, and settings stay put.
